Welcome, detective! Let us use numbers to prepare our school fair.

Two hundreds, three tens, and six ones make two hundred thirty-six. It is greater than two hundred thirty. Compare the place values, starting with the hundreds.

Addition joins amounts. Twenty-seven plus fifteen is forty-two. Exchange ten ones for one ten when needed. Subtraction takes an amount away. Forty-two minus fifteen returns us to twenty-seven. The operations help check each other.

Three bags with five pencils each make fifteen pencils. That is multiplication: equal groups. Sharing fifteen equally among three bags gives five each. That is division. Check that every bag has the same number.

Multiples follow equal jumps. Count in threes or fives. Fifteen appears in both patterns, so one number can fit two rules.

Fractions describe equal parts of a whole. One of four parts is one quarter. Two quarters make one half. Three shaded parts make three quarters.

Three thousand plus four thousand costs seven thousand rupiah. Pay ten thousand. How much change? Three thousand: subtract the cost from your payment.
