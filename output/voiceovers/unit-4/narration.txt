Charts turn information into clues. Let us read them!

Read the picture key first. If one symbol means two children, two symbols and a half mean five children. A different key may give one book symbol ten books. Then half a symbol means five books.

For bars, read the scale. Halfway between twelve and sixteen is fourteen. This bar shows fourteen visitors. Another shows eight. Subtract to find the difference: six more visitors.

A line chart tracks change over time. Read the dots against the height scale. Growing from four centimeters to nine centimeters means five centimeters of growth. Height and growth are different.

A pie chart shows parts of a whole. Thirty-two children split into eight equal parts means four per part. Half means sixteen children; one quarter means eight.
